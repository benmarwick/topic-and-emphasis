The city council voted on Tuesday to close the central library for eight months while builders replace the roof. Staff will move about a third of the books to a temporary branch in the old post office. Council members said the repair could not wait another winter, because leaks had already damaged two reading rooms. Some residents objected to the timing. They pointed out that students use the library most heavily in the months before examinations. The council promised longer opening hours at the temporary branch, although it has not yet said who will pay for them.
