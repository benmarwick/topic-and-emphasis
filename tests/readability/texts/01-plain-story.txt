The bus was late again. Mina stood at the stop and watched the rain fill the holes in the road. A dog ran past with a wet shoe in its mouth. She laughed, and the man next to her laughed too. When the bus came, they both got on and sat at the back. The driver said the bridge was shut, so the trip would take a long time. Mina did not mind. She had a book, a warm coat and no one to meet.
